How it works

One team. Many games. One finish line.

Your Grandmaster deals the challenges. Your team clears them together.

  1. 1

    Join a team

    Enter a code, choose a squad, and get ready.

  2. 2

    The Grandmaster deals the games

    Your Grandmaster assigns each teammate a role and a different challenge.

  3. 3

    Clear together

    Solve your own board. Clear early and you can wait, or risk it on a bonus round.

  4. 4

    Relay to the next level

    Your team advances only when everyone is cleared. First team to the finish wins.

The Grandmaster doesn't play. They command.

One player never touches a board. The Grandmaster watches the whole team, assigns roles and games, banks the currency everyone earns, and picks the moment to spend it on perks.

Learn about the Grandmaster

When the Duelist is called, the relay doesn't stop.

The Duelist skips the puzzles entirely and faces the rival team's champion in live head-to-head rounds while everyone else keeps racing. Four games, and you don't choose which one: the server does.

  • RPS Duel Pure prediction, five seconds a throw.
  • Crown Duel Five characters, and one hidden rewrite of your hand.
  • Number Clash Nine numbers, each spent once. Timing is everything.
  • Bid War Twenty coins, five secret auctions, every bid spent.
